You feel like you work without stopping and your money never lasts because you are trying to fill your life without putting God at the center. When we build our lives around our own plans and forget our Creator, things lose meaning and true blessing slips away. Constant exhaustion is often a clear sign that our priorities need an urgent reset.

Pull up a chair and take a warm sip of coffee with me. That heavy feeling of running on a hamster wheel is nothing new. Thousands of years ago, hardworking people just like us went through the very same frustrating struggle every single day.

### The Mystery of the Pockets with Holes

In the first chapter of Haggai in the Bible, God speaks to a group of everyday workers. They had returned home after years of hardship. They woke up early, worked until their hands were raw, but the numbers never added up at the end of the month.

Look at how Scripture describes their daily routine. They planted plenty of seed, but harvested very little. They ate meals, but never felt full. They put on clothes, but stayed cold. Worst of all, the wages they earned seemed to fall right through pockets that were torn and full of holes.

Do you recognize yourself in that picture? How often do we say: "It feels like my paycheck vanishes the moment it hits my account"? Or maybe you think: "I work until I drop, but this quiet emptiness inside my chest never seems to go away."

The problem was not laziness. Those people worked hard from sunrise to sunset. The real issue was where they put their attention. They pushed God off the throne of their hearts and placed their own personal comfort ahead of everything else in their lives.

### The Excuse That It Is Never the Right Time

God sent a message through an older prophet named Haggai. He asked with tender honesty: "Why do you have time and money to panel your own fancy houses, while the house of God sits broken down in ruins and completely forgotten?"

For them, the temple represented meeting with God, listening to His voice, and honoring Him together. But the people made excuses. They said: "Times are tough right now, the economy is bad, and this is simply not the right moment to focus on the things of God."

We do the very same thing today. We tell ourselves: "God understands how tired I am, so I will pray later." Or we say: "Once my bills are paid, then I will help others." We push God back until there is spare time on the calendar. But that spare time never comes. We were made to walk with our Creator, not run without Him.

### Stop and Look at Your Path

Twice in that short chapter, God tells the people: "Give careful thought to your ways." Picture a loving father sitting beside his worn-out child, asking gently: "Take a good look at where this constant rush is leading you. Is living this way really working out the way you hoped it would?"

The people thought their empty pockets were caused by bad weather or bad luck. But God showed them that He allowed their resources to leak away. He did not do this to be harsh, but out of love. Sometimes God lets our strength run dry so we will finally see that we cannot make it on our own.

The Bible calls this grace. Grace means receiving God's free gift of forgiveness, fresh hope, and unconditional love. Jesus came to carry our sins and heavy burdens on the cross, giving us redemption, which means freedom from guilt and fear. When we rely only on our own sweat and ignore Jesus, we end up stressed, weary, and empty.

### Climbing the Mountain to Start Over

The good news is that the people listened to God’s message. They did not become bitter or angry. They admitted their mistake, turned back to God, and changed. The Lord gave them a simple command: "Go up into the mountains, bring down wood, and rebuild my house."

They dropped their excuses and put God first. Right away, God gave them a comforting promise: "I am with you." When they put first things first, God’s blessing returned to their farms, their homes, and their hearts. Peace returned because God was back at the center of their lives.

If you want your work to bear fruit and your soul to rest, working more overtime is not the real answer. You need to reorder what comes first. Honor God before everything else, and you will see how He helps your daily life fall into place.

### A Practical Next Step

Tonight, before you turn on the screen or look at your phone, set aside ten quiet minutes. Sit on the edge of your bed, take a deep breath, and pray these honest words in your own voice:

"Lord, I am tired of carrying the weight of my life alone. I admit that I left You out of my plans. Tonight, I want to put things right. Please forgive me and take first place in my heart, my home, and my work."

Tomorrow morning, wake up five minutes earlier than usual. Spend those few minutes thanking God for a new day and asking for His peace before you begin your tasks. When you invite Him into your routine from the start, you will find that your burden becomes so much lighter.
