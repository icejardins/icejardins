You stop chasing other people’s approval when you realize that you are already fully loved and accepted by God. You do not need to wear yourself out trying to impress anyone to have true value in this life. The love of Jesus on the cross has already given you the greatest approval anyone could ever receive.

Pull up a chair and enjoy a warm cup of coffee with me. If you feel like you are always walking on eggshells just to please your boss, family, or friends, I understand your tired heart. Living for the opinions of others is like running on a treadmill that never stops. You run fast, end up exhausted, and never move forward an inch.

### The trap of living for an audience

Long ago, Paul wrote a letter to his friends in Galatia, found in Galatians chapter six. Back then, some religious leaders pressured people to follow rigid rituals. They did not care about helping hearts grow closer to God. They only wanted to look holy, win applause, and receive praise.

Paul saw through this selfish game and called it out with courage. He even picked up his pen and wrote in large letters to warn his friends about the danger of living only for appearances.

Human hearts have not changed much since then. We might not follow those ancient rituals, but we still find ways to show off. We buy expensive clothes, post edited photos, or show off new cars just to say, "Look at me! Look at how important I am!"

When we live like that, we become prisoners of what others think. If someone praises us, we feel on top of the world. But if people criticize our work or leave us out, our world crashes down. Chasing praise robs us of daily peace.

### Where your real value comes from

This is where the story of Jesus changes our lives. Paul said something that sounded foolish back then: his only boast was in the cross of Christ. Today, the cross is often seen as pretty jewelry. But back then, it was a place of terrible shame, where criminals were punished in public.

Why would anyone boast in a cross? Paul did because right there Jesus proved how precious you are to God. God did not wait for you to become perfect, rich, or popular before loving you. Jesus went to the cross for you with open arms, paying a heavy debt of mistakes we could never pay on our own.

This gift is what the Bible calls grace. Grace is deep kindness given freely to someone who cannot pay the bill. When you realize the Maker of the universe has already embraced you for free, the opinions of others lose all power to define who you are.

### The world loses its power over you

Paul said that because of the cross, the world was dead to him, and he was dead to the world. What does that mean on a Monday morning? It means the shiny promises and false pressures of this world can no longer fool you.

Think about what the world demands every day. It pushes us to chase money, keep up a flawless body, collect likes, and pretend to be perfect. But all of that fades quickly. When you grasp Jesus’ love, you see how empty those demands are. They no longer tell your heart what to do.

You do not have to wear a mask to fit in. If someone thinks you are foolish for choosing honesty, you can smile and let it go. If people leave you out because you refuse to gossip, you can sleep in peace. The cheers of the crowd no longer rule you, because God’s love already fills you up.

### A brand-new life from the inside out

Living with faith is not about following a boring list of strict rules. It is not about keeping cold habits to earn points with God or look like a saint. What truly matters is becoming an entirely new person on the inside.

When Jesus makes his home in our daily routine, He brings a fresh start. He sweeps away our envy, anger, and fear of rejection. In their place, He gives us peace, patience, and kindness. You begin to do good deeds not to earn praise, but simply because your heart overflows with joy and thanks.

Paul carried painful scars on his body because he stayed faithful to this message. He was never afraid of displeasing powerful leaders, because his life belonged to Christ alone. He knew who he was, and whose hands held his future.

### Your practical next step

Today, look at your daily routine and choose one simple decision that you usually make just to please other people or avoid criticism. It might mean saying a polite, honest "no" to an extra invitation that you do not have the time or energy to accept. It might mean putting your phone away instead of posting online just to check how many likes you get. Or it might mean walking away from buying something shiny that you do not need, simply to keep up with the neighbors. When you make that simple choice, take a slow, deep breath and whisper this quiet prayer: "Lord, Your love is enough for me. I do not need the praise of the world to know that I am forever Yours."
